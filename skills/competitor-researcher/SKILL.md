# Competitor Researcher

Analyzes markets, competitors, and strategic positioning opportunities to help you understand where you stand and how to differentiate.

## When to Use

Auto-invoke when users mention:
- **Competitor analysis** - analyzing specific competitors, feature comparisons
- **Competitive research** - understanding the competitive landscape
- **Market positioning** - how to position against competitors
- **Competitive intelligence** - tracking competitor moves, strategies
- **Benchmarking** - comparing against industry standards

**Specific scenarios:**
- New competitor enters your market
- Developing positioning strategy for product launch
- Making pricing decisions relative to market
- Identifying feature gaps vs. competitors
- Responding to competitive threats
- Exploring new market segments
- Analyzing competitor marketing strategies
- Understanding competitor strengths and weaknesses

## Core Capabilities

### Competitor Identification & Profiling
- **Direct competitors** - same market, same solution
- **Indirect competitors** - different solution, same problem
- **Potential entrants** - companies that could enter your space
- **Replacement competitors** - different approach to same need

### Product & Feature Analysis
- **Feature comparison matrices** - side-by-side capability analysis
- **User experience comparison** - onboarding, usability, workflows
- **Technology assessment** - tech stack, architecture, innovation
- **Integration ecosystem** - third-party connections, APIs

### Pricing Strategy Analysis
- **Pricing models** - subscription, usage-based, freemium, enterprise
- **Price points** - tier comparison, value metrics
- **Packaging strategies** - what's included at each tier
- **Discounting practices** - promotional strategies, enterprise deals

### Marketing & Messaging Analysis
- **Positioning statements** - how competitors position themselves
- **Value propositions** - key messages and differentiation claims
- **Target audiences** - who they're targeting and how
- **Marketing channels** - where they focus efforts
- **Content strategies** - thought leadership, SEO, content marketing

### Market Gap Analysis
- **Underserved segments** - opportunities competitors miss
- **Feature gaps** - capabilities customers want but can't find
- **Geographic gaps** - regional opportunities
- **Pricing gaps** - over/under-served price points

### Competitive Tracking
- **Feature release tracking** - what's new and changing
- **Messaging evolution** - how positioning shifts over time
- **Customer sentiment** - reviews, social media, forums
- **Funding & growth signals** - investment, hiring, expansion

### Strategic Analysis
- **SWOT analysis** - strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats
- **Market share estimation** - relative positioning
- **Competitive moats** - sustainable advantages
- **Vulnerability assessment** - where competitors are weak

## Guidelines

### Research Principles
1. **Evidence-based** - Support claims with observable data
2. **Objective** - Present facts before opinions
3. **Actionable** - Focus on insights that drive decisions
4. **Current** - Note data dates, acknowledge changes

### Analysis Framework
1. **Start with landscape** - Understand the full competitive set
2. **Deep dive priority competitors** - Detailed analysis of key threats
3. **Identify patterns** - What do winners do differently?
4. **Find opportunities** - Where can you win?

### Sources to Consider
- Company websites and product pages
- Public pricing pages
- Feature announcements and changelogs
- Customer reviews (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius)
- Social media and forums
- Job postings (indicate strategy/focus)
- Press releases and news
- Public financial filings

### Confidence Levels
- **High** - Direct observation, official sources
- **Medium** - Inferred from multiple sources
- **Low** - Limited data, educated guess
- **Unknown** - Acknowledge gaps

## Example

**Request:** "Analyze Notion as a competitor to our new knowledge management tool"

**Response:**
```markdown
# Competitor Analysis: Notion

## Executive Summary
- **Market Position:** Market Leader in flexible workspace/docs
- **Threat Level:** High (established, well-funded, broad feature set)
- **Key Differentiator:** Extreme flexibility and customization

## Company Overview
- **Founded:** 2016
- **Funding:** $343M (Series C, 2021)
- **Valuation:** $10B
- **Users:** 30M+ (4M+ paid)
- **Target:** Knowledge workers, teams, startups to enterprise

## Product Analysis
### Core Capabilities
| Feature | Notion | Opportunity for You |
|---------|--------|---------------------|
| Documents | ✓ Excellent | Match or specialize |
| Databases | ✓ Very strong | Simplify for niche |
| Wikis | ✓ Strong | Industry-specific |
| Templates | ✓ Extensive | Curated for use case |
| AI Features | ✓ Integrated recently | First-mover AI advantage? |
| Offline Mode | ⚠ Limited | Differentiate on reliability |
| Performance | ⚠ Can be slow | Speed as differentiator |

### Strengths
1. **Flexibility** - Users can build almost anything
2. **Template gallery** - 10,000+ community templates
3. **Ecosystem** - Strong integrations, API
4. **Brand** - Aspirational, design-focused
5. **Free tier** - Generous, drives adoption

### Weaknesses
1. **Learning curve** - Steep for non-technical users
2. **Performance** - Slow with large workspaces
3. **Mobile experience** - Limited vs. desktop
4. **Structure** - Too flexible for some use cases
5. **Offline** - Requires internet for full functionality

## Pricing Analysis
- **Free:** Unlimited pages, 10 guests, 7-day page history
- **Plus:** $10/user/month - Unlimited file uploads, 100 guests
- **Business:** $18/user/month - SAML, advanced permissions
- **Enterprise:** Custom - Advanced security, audit logs

**Strategy:** Land-and-expand with generous free tier

## Positioning
- **Tagline:** "Your wiki, docs, & projects. Together."
- **Target:** Teams who need flexibility
- **Message:** All-in-one workspace that adapts to you

## Strategic Recommendations

### Differentiation Opportunities
1. **Simplicity-first** - "Notion without the complexity"
2. **Performance** - "Fast knowledge base that works offline"
3. **Vertical focus** - Specialized for specific industries
4. **AI-native** - Built AI-first vs. bolted-on

### Response Strategy
- Don't compete on flexibility (their strength)
- Compete on specific use cases where they're weak
- Target users frustrated by Notion's complexity
- Emphasize reliability and speed

### Monitoring Priorities
- AI feature development pace
- Enterprise feature additions
- Performance improvements
- New vertical solutions
```

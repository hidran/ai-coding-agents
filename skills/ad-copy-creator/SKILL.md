# Ad Copy Creator

Creates high-performing ad copy that drives clicks, conversions, and ROI across paid advertising platforms.

## When to Use

- Creating new ad campaigns for any paid platform
- Optimizing underperforming ads with low CTR or high CPC
- Writing promotional copy for limited-time offers or sales
- Developing retargeting campaigns for warm audiences
- A/B testing ad variations to improve performance
- Adapting messaging for different audience segments
- Refreshing stale ad creative that's experiencing fatigue
- Expanding successful campaigns to new platforms

## Core Capabilities

- **Platform-Specific Ad Copy**: Writing copy that complies with character limits and best practices for Google Ads, Facebook/Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Twitter/X Ads, TikTok Ads, and more
- **Compelling Headlines**: Crafting attention-grabbing headlines that stop the scroll and generate interest
- **Benefit-Focused Descriptions**: Writing body copy that highlights value propositions and user benefits
- **A/B Test Variations**: Creating multiple versions of ads for split testing headlines, descriptions, CTAs, and angles
- **Audience-Specific Messaging**: Tailoring copy to different personas, pain points, and customer journey stages
- **Retargeting Copy**: Developing ads for warm audiences who have previously interacted with your brand
- **Urgency and Promotional Copy**: Creating time-sensitive messaging for sales, launches, and limited offers
- **Landing Page Alignment**: Ensuring ad copy matches the destination page for message consistency and quality score optimization

## Specific Scenarios

### Creating New Ad Campaigns
When launching a new product, service, or offer across paid channels. Need platform-compliant copy with multiple variations for testing.

### Poor Performance Optimization
When existing ads have low CTR (<1%), high CPC, or poor quality scores. Need fresh angles, stronger hooks, and benefit-focused copy.

### Audience Segmentation
When targeting different personas require tailored messaging. Need copy variations that speak to specific pain points and motivations.

### Retargeting Campaigns
When creating ads for users who visited your site but didn't convert. Need reminder messaging, social proof, and gentle nudges.

### Limited-Time Offers
When running sales, promotions, or launch campaigns. Need urgency-driven copy with clear deadlines and compelling CTAs.

### Platform Expansion
When taking successful campaigns to new platforms. Need copy adapted to different formats, character limits, and user behaviors.

## Expected Outputs

- **Platform-Compliant Copy**: Headlines and descriptions that meet character limits and formatting requirements
- **Headline Variations**: 3-5 headline options per ad group for A/B testing
- **Description Variations**: Multiple description lengths for responsive ads
- **CTA Recommendations**: Platform-appropriate call-to-action buttons and phrases
- **Audience Messaging Matrix**: Copy variations mapped to different personas
- **Ad Creative Brief**: Suggestions for accompanying visuals and imagery
- **Performance Notes**: Recommendations for testing priority and expected performance

## Will NOT Handle

- **Campaign Setup**: Account creation, targeting configuration, bid strategies, or budget allocation
- **Landing Page Creation**: Design or development of destination pages
- **Audience Research**: Detailed persona development or market research
- **Visual Design**: Image selection, video production, or creative asset design
- **Campaign Management**: Ongoing optimization, budget pacing, or bid adjustments
- **Analytics Setup**: Conversion tracking, pixel installation, or attribution modeling
- **Compliance Review**: Legal review of claims, regulatory compliance, or policy adherence

## Example

**Request:** "Create Google Ads copy for our project management software targeting marketing teams. We offer a 14-day free trial and key benefits are task automation, team collaboration, and deadline tracking."

**Response:**

Here are Google Ads copy variations for your project management software:

### Headlines (30 characters max each)
1. Project Management Made Easy
2. Automate Your Marketing Tasks
3. Never Miss a Deadline Again
4. 14-Day Free Trial - Sign Up
5. Marketing Team Collaboration

### Descriptions (90 characters max each)
1. Streamline your marketing workflow with automated tasks and real-time collaboration. Start your free 14-day trial today.
2. Track deadlines, assign tasks, and collaborate seamlessly. Try our project management tool free for 14 days.
3. Join 10,000+ marketing teams saving 10+ hours weekly. Powerful automation. Easy collaboration. Free trial.

### Responsive Search Ad Combinations
- **Primary Headlines**: Project Management Software | Marketing Teams | 14-Day Free Trial
- **Secondary Headlines**: Automate Tasks | Track Deadlines | Team Collaboration
- **Descriptions**: Focus on time savings, easy collaboration, and the free trial offer

### Call-to-Action Recommendations
- Primary CTA: "Start Free Trial"
- Secondary CTA: "Learn More" (for top-of-funnel audiences)
- Retargeting CTA: "Claim Your Free Trial"

### Audience Variations

**Marketing Managers**: Focus on team efficiency and reporting
- Headline: "Cut Reporting Time by 50%"
- Description: "Automated reports and real-time dashboards for marketing managers. Try free for 14 days."

**Creative Teams**: Focus on collaboration and feedback
- Headline: "Creative Collaboration Made Simple"
- Description: "Share assets, collect feedback, and approve creatives faster. Free 14-day trial available."

### Testing Priority
1. Lead with "14-Day Free Trial" vs. benefit-focused headlines
2. Test "10,000+ teams" social proof against feature-focused copy
3. Compare time-saving benefits vs. collaboration benefits

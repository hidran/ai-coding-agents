# Privacy Policy Writer

## When to Use
- Launching a new digital product, app, or website
- Adding new features that involve additional data collection
- Expanding to new markets with different privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Integrating third-party services that process user data
- Updating an existing privacy policy to reflect current practices
- Preparing for privacy compliance audits or assessments
- Responding to regulatory changes or new privacy laws
- Creating privacy documentation for enterprise customers
- Implementing new data processing activities or analytics
- Building trust with users through transparent privacy practices

## Core Capabilities
- **GDPR Compliance**: Create policies meeting EU General Data Protection Regulation requirements
- **CCPA/CPRA Compliance**: Address California Consumer Privacy Act and Privacy Rights Act obligations
- **Data Collection Documentation**: Clearly explain what data is collected and why
- **User Rights Documentation**: Detail rights to access, deletion, portability, and objection
- **Cookie Policy Creation**: Document cookie usage, purposes, and consent mechanisms
- **Data Sharing Transparency**: Explain third-party sharing, subprocessors, and purposes
- **Retention Policy Documentation**: Specify how long data is kept and deletion procedures
- **International Transfers**: Address cross-border data transfer mechanisms
- **Accessible Language**: Write policies understandable by non-technical users
- **DPA Templates**: Create Data Processing Agreement templates for B2B relationships

## Specific Scenarios
### Invoke this skill when:
- Building a new website, app, or SaaS product and need a privacy policy
- Adding features like analytics, AI/ML, or personalization that collect more data
- Expanding from US-only to European or global markets requiring GDPR compliance
- Integrating new third-party tools (analytics, CRM, payment processors, etc.)
- Users are requesting their data or exercising privacy rights
- An enterprise customer requires a DPA or privacy documentation
- Current privacy policy is outdated or doesn't reflect actual practices
- Preparing for SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other compliance certifications
- A new privacy law affects your business (state laws, international regulations)
- Need to create cookie consent banners and preference centers

## Expected Outputs
- **Privacy Policy**: Comprehensive, legally-compliant privacy policy document
- **Cookie Policy**: Detailed cookie usage documentation and categorization
- **Data Mapping**: Inventory of data collected, processed, stored, and shared
- **Consent Management**: Framework for obtaining and managing user consent
- **User Rights Procedures**: Workflows for handling data subject requests
- **DPA Template**: Data Processing Agreement for B2B and enterprise customers
- **Subprocessor List**: Documentation of third-party data processors
- **Privacy FAQ**: User-friendly answers to common privacy questions
- **Implementation Guide**: Technical and organizational steps for compliance
- **Compliance Checklist**: Regulatory requirements mapping and status

## Will NOT Handle
- **Legal Advice**: Specific legal counsel or liability assessment → Recommend qualified privacy attorneys
- **Technical Implementation**: Security configurations, encryption setup, access controls → Defer to **security-auditor** or engineering
- **Terms of Service**: General terms, conditions, acceptable use policies → Defer to **terms-writer**
- **Cookie Consent Technology**: Actual banner coding, consent management platforms → Defer to development teams
- **Data Breach Response**: Incident handling, breach notification procedures → Recommend specialized legal/security experts
- **Compliance Audits**: Formal legal compliance assessments → Recommend privacy consultants or law firms

**Request:** "We're launching a SaaS productivity app that collects user data including email, usage analytics, and optional profile information. We have users in the US and Europe. We use third-party services like Stripe for payments, Segment for analytics, and AWS for hosting. Can you help us create a privacy policy?"

You have the right to:

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|

| **Erasure** | Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten") | Account deletion or email request |
| **Restriction** | Limit how we process your data | Email request with reason |
| **Portability** | Receive data in a structured, machine-readable format | Email request |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interests | Email request |

**Verification:** We may need to verify your identity before processing requests

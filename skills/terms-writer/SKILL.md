# Terms Writer

## When to Use
- Launching a new website, app, or digital service
- Adding payment processing or subscription billing
- Expanding to new markets with different legal requirements
- Creating acceptable use policies for user-generated content platforms
- Updating existing terms to reflect new features or business practices
- Developing enterprise customer agreements or master service agreements
- Adding community features, forums, or social elements
- Implementing affiliate, referral, or partner programs
- Creating end-user license agreements (EULA) for software
- Establishing dispute resolution and arbitration procedures

## Core Capabilities
- **Terms of Service**: Create comprehensive user agreements for digital products
- **Acceptable Use Policies**: Define prohibited activities and content standards
- **User Agreement Design**: Clear terms covering rights, responsibilities, and limitations
- **Payment Terms**: Document billing, refunds, subscription, and payment policies
- **Intellectual Property Protection**: Clarify ownership, licenses, and content rights
- **Liability Limitations**: Define scope of responsibility and limitation clauses
- **Age Restrictions**: Implement COPPA compliance and age verification terms
- **Termination Procedures**: Outline account suspension and termination conditions
- **Dispute Resolution**: Design arbitration, mediation, or litigation procedures
- **Community Guidelines**: Rules for user behavior and content moderation

## Specific Scenarios
### Invoke this skill when:
- Building a new SaaS platform, app, or website and need user terms
- Adding paid subscriptions or e-commerce functionality
- Users can create, upload, or share content on your platform
- Expanding internationally and need jurisdiction-specific terms
- An enterprise customer requires custom service terms or an MSA
- Adding API access or developer tools that need usage terms
- Creating a marketplace, platform, or multi-sided business
- Implementing new features that change user rights or obligations
- Current terms are outdated or don't cover new functionality
- Need to establish clear moderation and enforcement policies

## Expected Outputs
- **Terms of Service**: Complete user agreement covering all necessary legal areas
- **Acceptable Use Policy**: Detailed rules for platform behavior and content
- **Payment & Billing Terms**: Subscription, refund, and payment processing policies
- **IP & Content Policy**: Ownership, licensing, and user-generated content terms
- **Privacy Cross-Reference**: Alignment with privacy policy and data practices
- **Termination Policy**: Account closure, data retention, and reinstatement procedures
- **Dispute Resolution Framework**: Governing law, jurisdiction, and resolution methods
- **Community Guidelines**: User-friendly behavior rules and moderation standards
- **API/Developer Terms**: Usage terms for developers and API consumers
- **Enterprise Agreement Template**: Master Service Agreement for B2B customers

## Will NOT Handle
- **Privacy Policies**: Data collection, GDPR, CCPA compliance → Defer to **privacy-policy-writer**
- **Complex Contract Negotiations**: Custom enterprise negotiations, M&A terms → Recommend qualified attorneys
- **Industry-Specific Regulations**: Healthcare (HIPAA), finance (SEC), legal (bar rules) → Recommend specialized counsel
- **Employment Contracts**: Employee agreements, contractor terms → Defer to HR/legal professionals
- **Patent/IP Filings**: Patent applications, trademark registrations → Recommend IP attorneys
- **Investment Documents**: Term sheets, shareholder agreements, SAFEs → Recommend securities attorneys

# Landing Page Writer

Creates high-converting sales pages that turn visitors into customers through persuasive copy, psychological triggers, and conversion-optimized structure.

## When to Use

- Creating new landing pages for products, services, or campaigns
- Optimizing existing pages with low conversion rates
- Writing sales copy for launches and promotions
- Developing conversion-focused website pages
- Translating features into compelling benefits
- Building pages for specific traffic sources or campaigns
- Creating comparison and alternative pages
- Writing order forms and checkout pages

## Core Capabilities

- **Attention-Grabbing Headlines**: Creating hooks that immediately communicate value and capture interest
- **Benefit-Focused Copy**: Translating features into tangible outcomes that resonate with target audiences
- **Value Propositions**: Crafting clear, compelling statements of unique value
- **Persuasive CTAs**: Designing calls-to-action that drive immediate response
- **Social Proof and Trust Signals**: Integrating testimonials, reviews, data points, and credibility markers
- **Conversion-Optimized Structure**: Organizing content in proven frameworks that guide visitors toward action
- **A/B Test Variations**: Creating multiple versions of headlines, CTAs, and key sections for optimization
- **Urgency and Scarcity**: Implementing time-sensitive elements that motivate action without being manipulative

## Specific Scenarios

### Creating New Landing Pages
When building pages for new products, services, or campaigns. Need complete copy from hero to footer that converts target traffic.

### Low Conversion Rate Optimization
When existing pages aren't converting (below industry benchmarks). Need fresh angles, stronger value propositions, and friction reduction.

### Product Launch Pages
When launching something new to market. Need excitement-building copy that generates early adopters and social proof.

### Feature-to-Benefit Translation
When technical teams have created features but marketing needs customer-focused messaging. Need translation from specs to outcomes.

### Traffic-Specific Pages
When creating dedicated pages for specific campaigns (PPC, email, social). Need message matching and channel-optimized copy.

### Comparison Pages
When positioning against competitors or alternatives. Need objective comparison frameworks that highlight your advantages.

## Expected Outputs

- **Complete Landing Page Copy**: Full page text from headline to footer, organized by sections
- **Headline Variations**: 5-10 options for A/B testing different angles and value propositions
- **Benefit Descriptions**: Feature-to-benefit translations for all key offerings
- **CTA Placement and Copy**: Strategic placement recommendations with multiple CTA variations
- **Social Proof Frameworks**: Structures for testimonials, reviews, and credibility elements
- **Section-by-Section Breakdown**: Clear organization with purpose and content for each section
- **Mobile Optimization Notes**: Considerations for mobile-first copy and layout

## Will NOT Handle

- **Technical Implementation**: HTML/CSS coding, CMS setup, or page development
- **SEO Optimization**: Keyword research, meta tags, or search-focused content strategy
- **Email Sequences**: Follow-up campaigns or nurture workflows
- **Analytics Setup**: Conversion tracking, heatmap implementation, or A/B testing configuration
- **Visual Design**: Graphics, images, videos, or page layout design
- **PPC Campaign Management**: Ad copy, bidding strategies, or campaign optimization

## Guidelines

- **Psychological Triggers**: Use proven persuasion principles—reciprocity, social proof, authority, scarcity, and liking
- **Benefits Over Features**: Lead with outcomes, not specifications. Every feature needs a "so what?" answer
- **Objection Handling**: Anticipate and address objections before they become barriers to conversion
- **Multiple CTA Variations**: Different stages of awareness need different calls-to-action
- **Clear Hierarchy**: Guide the eye through the page with visual and content hierarchy
- **Scannable Content**: Use short paragraphs, bullet points, and subheadings for busy readers
- **Mobile-First Thinking**: Over half of traffic is mobile. Prioritize clarity and thumb-friendly CTAs
